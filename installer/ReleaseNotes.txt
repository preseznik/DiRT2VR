DiRT2VR — Experimental

Xbox preset now fills missing keyboard controls with the game's default keys and keeps your custom keys. If keyboard controls were left blank by an earlier preset, apply Xbox preset again and Save driving controls.

VR preparation now handles read-only camera and effect files. If an older build failed on a read-only cameras.xml file, update, choose Restore original files and retry.

Chase-camera Free look now hides the Windows mouse pointer over the game picture. Pause, menus, other camera views and switching apps restore it. Window borders keep their usual cursor.

With chase-camera Free look enabled, scroll forward to move closer or backward to move farther away. No mouse button is needed. Changing chase views resets the distance. Desktop zoom is player-tested; headset checks remain pending.

Choose Hold left, Hold right or Always on for mouse look under Controls → General → Chase camera. The three mouse-icon buttons show your choice. Hold right remains the default; save settings and relaunch to apply.

Hold the right stick pressed in to look behind the car while chase-camera Free look is enabled. Release it to return to the normal chase view.

The launcher now remembers its window size and maximized state when you close it. No need to resize it every time.

New: Controls is organized into General, VR, Driving and Seat tabs. Find cockpit animation and chase-camera settings in General, VR shortcuts in VR, driving bindings in Driving, and seat shortcuts in Seat.

New: Experimental chase-camera free look. Move the mouse using your chosen activation mode, or use the right stick, to look around your car. Your angle stays while stopped and smoothly returns behind the car when driving. Adjust mouse/stick sensitivity and Invert vertical in Controls → General. Free look starts off; save and relaunch after enabling it. Desktop requires DX11. VR also needs Advanced → Graphics → 3D beyond the cockpit; headset testing remains pending.

New: Mizu Mountain in CUSTOM tracks. Build the full 10.4 km daylight route from your own GRID 2 installation, then Launch or Launch VR for Direct practice or experimental Race against up to seven opponents. Choose opponent cars and difficulty as usual. Already installed Mizu? Use Manage → Rebuild from source for the starting grid and MIZU pre-race label. Each race covers the route once; VR is experimental and LAN remains unavailable.

New: Graphics → Filters lets you create and share your own visual presets.

Choose separate presets for Desktop and VR, with Day and Night adjustments for colour, bloom and exposure. Original keeps the game's look. Save settings and relaunch to apply; Main retains the existing graphics controls and Bloom switches.

Choosing, creating or importing a preset in the editor selects it for both Desktop and VR. You can still change the two launch selectors separately afterward.

Presets can be imported and exported as DiRT2VR JSON files. Changes are restored after play. Initial desktop use is player-approved; headset rendering and differing presets in LAN remain unverified.
