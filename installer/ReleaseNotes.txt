Create a new career without changing your existing save.

- New Profile tab: choose your current career or create a separate Fresh or 100% completed career.
- Completed careers include stock events, cars, liveries, rewards, All-Star upgrades and teammate relationships.
- Your selection applies to desktop, VR and LAN launches, including Return to menus.
- Graphics settings and launcher controls stay shared between careers.
- New names accept letters A-Z and numbers 0-9 only, avoiding display problems in-game.
- Delete launcher-created profiles with confirmation. Your current game career is protected.

Newly created careers become active automatically. The active row is green and marked Active. Use this profile switches to an existing career. Close the game before switching profiles.

Some save details, including garage counts and intermediate completion percentages, currently show Unavailable. Existing careers cannot be unlocked by this feature.
