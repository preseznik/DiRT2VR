DiRT2VR 0.17.23 - Experimental test build

More consistent trackside detail in VR
Tire stacks, flags and scaffolding now retain detail farther away, with room to draw more of these objects. This candidate targets the reported pop-in on Hammada Circuit.

The change applies automatically to VR launches. Desktop rendering is unchanged. It may cost performance; please compare the same section of track. Headset confirmation is still pending, and some track-specific flickering may remain.

The accepted VR cockpit-steering improvement is retained. On affected PC1060 setups, keep Remove artificial steering corrections off while its launch crash is investigated.

This installer is for local testing and has not been published.
