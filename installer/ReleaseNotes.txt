DiRT2VR 0.17.41 — Experimental

CUSTOM-track progress is now larger and green. Use Stop build beside the message to safely stop checking or conversion. Installed tracks are kept; final installation is allowed to finish.

Choose Race difficulty for stock and CUSTOM tracks: Easy, Casual, Serious, Savage, Extreme or Hardcore. Use game setting keeps your saved choice. Each custom location remembers its own difficulty.

Driving controls now shows live controller, wheel and pedal readings, including axes, buttons and POV hats. Choose Refresh after connecting a device. This display does not change your bindings.

Includes the current Aspen and Smelter layouts, Race options and car choices, plus Up/Down navigation in direct-session pause menus. No track rebuild is required for these launcher changes.

This is an opt-in Experimental build. New race difficulty and live input feedback need wider gameplay and hardware testing.
