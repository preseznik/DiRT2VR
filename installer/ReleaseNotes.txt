DiRT2VR 0.17.23 - Experimental

More consistent trackside detail in VR
Tire stacks, flags and scaffolding now retain detail farther away, with room to draw more of these objects. User testing in 0.17.23 confirmed that the reported Hammada Circuit pop-in appears resolved.

The change applies automatically to VR launches. Desktop rendering is unchanged. It may cost performance; please compare the same section of track. Other tracks may still have separate visibility or flickering issues.

The accepted VR cockpit-steering improvement is retained. The previously reported PC1060 VR launch crash could no longer be reproduced in 0.17.22 and is marked resolved.
