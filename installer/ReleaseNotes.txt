DiRT2VR 0.17.17 - Experimental test build

Cockpit steering animation
Try Controls > Cockpit animation > Remove artificial steering corrections (Experimental). It is off by default. Enable it, save settings, then Launch VR.

This candidate targets extra wheel and hand twitch in the player's cockpit. It keeps the game's original animation range and steering response. It does not provide full 540/900-degree wheel matching.

Please compare slow turns and rough-road driving with the option off and on. Check both hands and wheel, shifting and handbrake movements. Driving and headset validation are still pending. Turn the option off and relaunch if anything looks wrong.

This is a local testing installer, not a published release.
