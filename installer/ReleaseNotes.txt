DiRT2VR 0.17.20 - Experimental test build

Direct launches and sign-in
Practice and Race now check sign-in before loading your career. If already signed in, you continue straight to the event. Otherwise, GFWL asks you to sign in first. This candidate addresses the PC3080 startup freeze; it still needs confirmation on that machine.

Canceling sign-in or a reported career-load error ends the launch and restores temporary files. Your saved career is not replaced or reset.

Testing
Try desktop Practice with Current game career, signing into your usual local GFWL profile if prompted. Then check that a launcher-created Fresh career still starts without a manual sign-in. Diagnostic logging is optional; no memory dump is needed.

Cockpit steering animation
The reported missing hands and static wheel remain under investigation. Keep Remove artificial steering corrections off for normal play. This build does not claim a steering-animation fix.

This installer is for local testing; it has not been published as a release.
