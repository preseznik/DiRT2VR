DiRT2VR — Experimental

New: separate Bloom switches in Graphics for Desktop and VR rendering.

Turn Bloom off to remove the soft glow around bright areas while keeping colour grading, exposure and shadows. Both switches start On, preserving your existing look. Save settings and relaunch to apply.

Your choice applies to Normal Launch, Practice, Race, custom tracks and multiplayer HOST/JOIN. VR's choice also applies to the flat virtual screen. Original effects are restored after play.
