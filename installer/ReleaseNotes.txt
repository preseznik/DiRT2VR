Try shadows in VR.

- New Shadows (experimental) switch in Graphics > VR rendering.
- Shadows start off, keeping your current appearance and performance.
- Enable them to try your saved game shadow quality with corrected sunlight shadow placement for VR eyes.
- Desktop play and your original game settings are preserved.

Headset appearance and performance still need testing. If shadows look wrong or reduce performance, turn the option off and relaunch.
