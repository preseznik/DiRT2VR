DiRT2VR 0.17.22 - Experimental test build

Smoother cockpit steering in VR
This candidate keeps steering correction active between VR frames, targeting the slight wheel judder that remained after the desktop improvement.

To try it, enable Controls > Cockpit animation > Remove artificial steering corrections. Leave Observe only unchecked, save settings and relaunch. Compare slow turns and rough ground in the same car and event.

The option stays off by default. It preserves handling, force feedback and the original animation range; it does not provide full 540/900-degree wheel matching. Headset confirmation of this change is still needed.

The reported PC1060 VR crashes remain under investigation. This build does not contain a confirmed crash fix.

This installer is for local testing and has not been published.
