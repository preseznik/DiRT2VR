DiRT2VR — Experimental

Add prepared community tracks and cars in DiRT2VR/custom-tracks/external or DiRT2VR/custom-cars/external, then choose Refresh. Tracks appear under External; cars appear in CUSTOM cars. Missing or incompatible packs show an explanation. Original game files are restored after play. This accepts prepared DiRT2VR packs, not raw assets from other games.

Import Ferrari F2004 from your local Assetto Corsa installation using CUSTOM cars → Build/import. It currently supports desktop Direct practice on Battersea Air and Nordschleife Daylight. VR, AI races, LAN, career and saved replay playback are not available for this car yet.

Saved replays remember the exact external packs they need. Restore the same pack version before playback. Fix a playback failure affecting recordings with empty animation-name tables.

Find CUSTOM tracks faster with the Source dropdown: check one or more source games, or show them all. Installed tracks only hides layouts you haven't built yet. Save settings remembers your filters; tracks needing repair remain visible.

Full-race recording now starts OFF. To use Full Replay or save new replays, turn ON Record full replays in Launcher → Replays before launching. Instant replay, rewind and your existing saved replays still work with it off.

Fixed flickering black bands on Misty Loch's road while retaining its surface detail. After updating, select Misty Loch → Manage → Rebuild from source once to apply the fix. Existing replays for the same track version remain compatible.

New: Large Address Aware under Advanced → Memory gives DiRT 2 more address space on 64-bit Windows and may help crashes when changing races. Off by default; the original executable is restored after play. This is experimental, not a confirmed fix for every crash.

Fixed Lakeside and other Aspen night layouts being blocked at launch after rebuilding. No new track rebuild is required.

New Controls → Replay settings let you bind playback and camera controls. In chase replays, move the Xbox left stick up/down to zoom; Page Up/Page Down and the mouse wheel also work. Enable chase-camera Free look under Controls → General.

Fixed custom tracks asking for another rebuild after enabling visual filters or disabling Bloom. If you already rebuilt your track, you can launch it without rebuilding again.

Required custom-track rebuilds now appear above the launch settings in bold green text with a gentle pulse, making it clear why launching is unavailable.

New: Save completed Direct practice and Race replays in Launcher → Replays. Play them later on desktop or in VR, or turn on Auto-save completed runs. Files are compressed, and the launcher checks that the recorded cars and track are available before starting playback. Saved replays currently work only in the installation that recorded them; sharing is not supported yet.

Full Replay is now available in the finish menu. Watch from the start, pause with Esc or controller B, and resume with the same button or Continue. Chase-camera orbit and zoom remain available. VR uses the existing 3D chase replays setting; cinematic angles stay on the flat screen.

Fixed jerky replay car movement when frame-rate-independent rewind is enabled.

New: Keep running when unfocused under Advanced → Gameplay. Enable it to keep playback running when switching apps; manual pause still works. Off by default. Use windowed or borderless mode.

New: Vehicle setup beside the car selection. Adjust gear ratio, downforce, suspension, ride height, differential and brake bias for stock and custom Direct practice and Race launches.

Misty Loch now supports VR practice and experimental desktop/VR races with up to seven opponents and twenty laps. Rebuild an existing Misty Loch pack to add the starting grid.

The launcher now clears older temporary installers, abandoned track-build files and diagnostic logs while idle. Settings, saved replays, profiles, installed tracks and recovery backups are kept.
