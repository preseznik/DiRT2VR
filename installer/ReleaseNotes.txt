EARLIER RELEASES
Highlights only; the latest changes are listed above.

0.17.145 — Experimental (changes since 0.17.143)

- Pikes Peak in VR: enables VR practice, Rally and Grid race on all four layouts—tarmac and the three gravel conditions. Existing prepared packs require version 1.0.8 or later.
- Build Pikes Peak from your own DiRT Rally installation: select CUSTOM tracks → Source: DiRT Rally → Pikes Peak → Build and install…, then choose your source game folder and desired layouts. Existing prepared packs and their saved replays are preserved.

Track assets are built locally and aren’t included in the download. LAN remains unavailable for Pikes Peak, and uneven crowd lighting remains a known issue.

0.17.143 — Experimental (changes since 0.17.135)

- Prepared Pikes Peak packs in the normal launcher, with desktop practice, staggered Rally and Grid race, plus Full Replay recording, finish-menu playback and saved replays.
- Seven more stock cars: MG Metro 6R4, Dallenbach Special, Ford RS200 Evolution, Mitsubishi Pajero Dakar 1993, Toyota Stadium Truck, 1995 Subaru Impreza WRX STI and Dodge Power Wagon.
- Keep your chosen chase-camera angle and zoom out up to 1000% of normal. Filter stock-car pickers by class.
- Fix VR seat controls becoming unavailable during headset startup.
- Optional Pikes Peak distance-blur control and clearer warnings for unsupported external-track replay settings.

0.17.135 — Experimental (changes since 0.17.131)

- Smoother Misty Loch roads: updated road and pit surfaces to reduce excessive shaking.
- Misty Loch VR sun fix: corrected the remaining sun drift as you move.
- Ferrari F2004 in VR: restored engine sound that follows RPM and sequential shifting in Direct practice and Race.
- Ferrari F2004 suspension: fixed the rear upper suspension arms spinning with the wheels.

After updating: use Manage → Rebuild from source for Misty Loch and the Ferrari F2004. Older Misty Loch replays need their original track build.

Driving confirmation of the smoother roads and a headset retest of Ferrari audio/shifting are still pending. Please report any problems.

0.17.131 — Experimental (changes since 0.17.120)

- Ferrari F2004 improvements: experimental grid races on compatible tracks, steadier V10 sound through throttle changes and shifts, and a steering-wheel display showing RPM, gear and speed.
- Choose stock-car upgrades: select Rookie, Pro or All-Star for the whole Direct practice or Race grid. New saved replays and best-lap records remember the grade.
- Longer shadow range: optional 160–640 m distances for desktop, plus Also apply in VR when VR shadows are enabled. VR’s nearby Detailed distance remains separate. Save and relaunch to apply.
- VR scenery fixes: correct the sun drifting with the camera and reduce grass popping in dense scenery.
- Nordschleife HQ road markings: corrected lighting on graffiti, painted lines and tyre marks.
- More flexible shifting: F2004 sessions use sequential shifting without changing your saved game setting. An experimental option lets manual cars use sequential and H-pattern inputs together.
- Fix intermittent custom-car preparation failures. Disabled launch buttons now explain their restrictions when you hover over them.

After updating: use Manage → Rebuild from source for the Ferrari and high-quality Nordschleife to apply their asset changes.

F2004 races, car grades and combined shifters still need wider driving and headset testing. AI opponents in F2004 races still use DiRT 2 engine sounds. The extended VR shadow range awaits headset testing; longer ranges can reduce sharpness and performance. Nordschleife Evening also awaits a visual retest.

0.17.120 — Experimental (changes since 0.17.118)

- Race start defaults to native staggered starts on stock Rally and Trailblazer stages. Choose Grid race to start everyone together. CUSTOM tracks retain grid starts. Spillway Short passed desktop acceptance; VR still needs testing.
- Newly saved replays restore particle effects correctly when reopened. Older recordings missing that data stop with an error instead of crashing and must be recorded again.
- Optional 3D trailer menus with head tracking. Enable Advanced → Graphics → 3D menus, then save and relaunch VR. Loading screens remain flat; this experimental option starts off.
- Quit is now available at the end of Direct practice and Race, including when Full Replay recording is off or unavailable. It exits the game without reopening the menus.

0.17.118 — Experimental (changes since 0.17.113)

- Better-looking Nordschleife: optional high quality assets add more natural lighting, more detailed surfaces and trackside scenery shadows in Daylight, Overcast and Evening.
- Mizu Mountain VR fix: distant mountain sections no longer disappear as you look around. No track rebuild is needed for this fix.
- Ferrari F2004 audio: corrected engine pitch through gear shifts and throttle changes. After updating, choose CUSTOM cars → Manage → Rebuild from source to apply the fix.
- Fixed an error when choosing Rebuild from source or another action in the CUSTOM car Manage menu.

To try Nordschleife's improved visuals, choose CUSTOM tracks → Nordschleife → Manage → Rebuild from source and enable Build with high quality assets. Standard remains the default, and your best laps are retained. High quality can use more GPU power and memory.

0.17.113 — Experimental
- Optional high quality Misty Loch assets, improved lighting and shadows, and fixes for overly bright grass and changing mountain lighting.
- Separate Desktop and VR controls for Misty Loch road shine.
- Smoother Ferrari F2004 gamepad steering and less abrupt grip loss. Handling remains experimental.
- Rebuild Misty Loch and the Ferrari from Manage to apply these asset changes.

0.17.112 — Experimental

- Bring the game window forward during VR startup, including with VDXR.
